# Discovery Call Script — Agency Owners (B2B Focus)

## Your Positioning
You help **agencies with B2B clients** get more meetings with their ideal prospects using LinkedIn automation — so they can scale without doing manual outreach.

---

## Opening (30 seconds)
"Hey [Name], thanks for making time! I'm Val. Quick question — what's the main challenge you're facing right now when it comes to getting new clients for your agency?"

## Listen & Mirror (2 min)
Let them talk. Then mirror back:
"So what I'm hearing is [summarize: e.g., 'you're spending too much time on outreach and want a more predictable flow of leads'] — is that accurate?"

## Qualify (ask these)
- "How many clients are you currently working with?"
- "What's your average client value?"
- "What's your current lead flow look like — inbound, outbound, referrals?"

## The Pitch (once qualified)
"That makes total sense. Here's what I do: I help agencies like yours automate qualified outreach on LinkedIn — so you're getting B2B decision-makers to raise their hand for a meeting, without spending hours manually messaging.

The goal is predictable meetings with your ideal prospects, so you can focus on delivering results."

## The Close
"Does that sound like something that would help where you are right now?"

---

## Common Objections

**"I already have a freelancer doing outreach"**
→ "Nice! What's been the biggest challenge with that? And are they getting you meetings, or just conversations?"

**"We tried LinkedIn before and it didn't work"**
→ "Totally get it — what was the approach? A lot of people 'spray and pray' and get burned out. What I do is different because [targeted, personalized, focus on raise-your-hand offers]."

**"Can you just send me more info?"**
→ "Absolutely — what's the one question I can answer that would help you decide if this is a fit?"

## Key Differentiators to Mention
- You focus on B2B (not generalist)
- Automations save time vs manual outreach
- Focus on "meetings with ideal prospects" not just "leads"
- Raise-the-hand approach = qualified, not pushy
