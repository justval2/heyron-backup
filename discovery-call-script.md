# Discovery Call Script

## Opening (30 seconds)
"Hey [Name], thanks for booking a call! I'm [Your Name]. Before we dive in, want to give me a quick 30-second overview of what you're working on and what's prompting you to look into [service] right now?"

## Listen & Mirror (2 min)
Let them talk. Then mirror back:
"So what I'm hearing is [summarize their problem]. Is that accurate?"

## Qualify & Educate
"If that fits, here's what I typically see working for [their type of business]..."

## The Close
"What I recommend is [outline]. Does that resonate with what you were hoping to hear?"

## Handle Objections
- **"I need to think about it"** → "Totally makes sense. What specifically do you want to think through?"
- **"Can you send more info?"** → "Sure — what's the one thing that would help you make a decision?"

---

## Pro Tips
- Don't pitch until they ask or you've qualified them
- Ask "what prompted you to look into this now?" — reveals urgency
- If they're not a fit, say so — builds trust
